# Support

For usage questions or reproducible non-sensitive defects, use
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/Arnon-hs/atlas-engine/issues). Include
`atlas-engine version`, platform, command, relevant bounds, and a minimal public
or synthetic example. Do not attach your repository, absolute local paths,
customer data, or secrets. Reduce logs and review them before sharing.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue forms.

Support is best effort, with no response-time guarantee, commercial SLA, or
guaranteed release calendar. Consumer infrastructure, scheduling, embeddings,
databases and publication are outside this project's support boundary. Start
with [the CLI reference](docs/contracts/cli.md) and the
[subprocess integration guide](docs/integrations/subprocess-consumers.md).
